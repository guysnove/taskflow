# Teams in TaskFlow: what's coming, and when

*For the studio owner and the team lead at Brightside. No technical knowledge needed. Course 2, Chapter 23.*

**What you asked for.** Last month two client deadlines were missed because a task sat in one person's list while
they were away. You asked for shared work that everyone on the team can see, and that nobody outside the team can.

**What we're building.** A "team" in TaskFlow. Maya creates it and invites the studio. Any task can belong to the team:
everyone in it sees it, and Maya can give it to one person, who is told straight away. When someone leaves the team,
their team tasks go back to the shared pile instead of disappearing with them. Maya gets one page with every open
team task, grouped by person, so she can rebalance work before someone goes on leave.

**How we keep client work private.** Think of each team as a locked room. We're building one door and one lock
for it, rather than a lock on every cupboard, because one lock is easier to check. Before the feature is switched on,
we'll test that someone outside the team can't open a team task, through every way into TaskFlow.

**When.** In about six weeks (three two-week rounds of work), most likely mid-January. This is a forecast, not a promise:
we've never measured this team's pace on this kind of work before. After the first two weeks we'll know much
more, and we'll tell you then whether mid-January still holds.

**What's not included.** Comments on tasks, and chat. We left them out to deliver the important part sooner.

**What we need from you.** One answer: should someone be able to move one of their own tasks into the team? (Yes or no is fine.)
